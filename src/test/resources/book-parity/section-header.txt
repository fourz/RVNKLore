§8§o◆ History

Ravenkraft began as a handful of farms on the shore of a cold lake. The first settlers built in spruce and cobble, and they argued about everything — the shape of the harbour, the height of the walls, the name of the town itself. Records from that time are thin; most were lost when the archive flooded.

What survives tells of a council of seven, a bell that rang at every dusk, and a long feud with the miners of the eastern ridge. The feud ended with a wedding, or so the songs claim. The bell still hangs in the old tower, cracked but unbowed, and the council still meets on the first day of each season to read the names of the founders aloud.
